Tandu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Tandu is a free app and website for picking and playing activities with your kids. We built it to be private by default: no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no collection of personal information from you or your children.

What we collect

Only anonymous, aggregate usage analytics, to understand which activities are used and to improve the app. This is collected via PostHog, hosted in the European Union, and is limited to:

  • Anonymous interaction events — which screens and activities are opened, and taps within the app.
  • Basic technical context — app version, browser/device type, screen size, language.

What we do not collect or do

  • No names, emails, accounts, or contact details.
  • No precise or IP-based location — client IP addresses are discarded and not stored.
  • No persistent identifiers — analytics uses in-memory, per-session identifiers only, so we cannot recognise a returning user or device.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no cross-site or cross-app tracking.
  • No session recording and no profiling of individuals.

Children's privacy

Tandu is intended to be used by families and is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or from anyone). Our analytics is anonymous and aggregate and is not used to identify, profile, or advertise to any user. This is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR rules for children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Playing online with others

Some activities let two devices play together. When you do this, Tandu connects your device directly to the other player's device to exchange game moves so you can play in real time. These moves are used only to run the game between the two devices — we do not store them, and you are not asked for any personal information to play.

Data retention

Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog under their standard retention and cannot be traced back to an individual. Because we hold no personal data, there is no personal account or profile to delete.

Third parties

Our only third-party processor is PostHog (analytics, EU region). See the PostHog privacy policy. We do not use advertising or social-media SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
